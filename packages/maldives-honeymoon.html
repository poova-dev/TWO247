Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 01

Arrival in Male & Scenic Seaplane Transfer

Arrive at Velana International Airport (MLE). Board your scenic seaplane or speedboat transfer over turquoise atolls to your luxury island resort. Check into your private water villa, receive a welcome bottle of sparkling wine, and unwind watching the sun dip into the ocean.

Day 02

Coral Reef Snorkeling & Sunset Dolphin Cruise

Start with a floating breakfast in your private pool. In the afternoon, embark on a guided house reef snorkeling expedition to observe sea turtles, rays, and tropical fish. As dusk arrives, enjoy a traditional Maldivian Dhoni cruise to spot spinner dolphins.

Day 03

Couple Spa Experience & Private Sandbank Dinner

Indulge in an overwater couple massage using aromatic tropical oils. At sunset, take a private boat to a secluded desert sandbank for an exclusive 4-course candlelight dinner illuminated by lanterns and stars.

Day 04

Morning Lagoon Swim & Departure Transfer

Enjoy your final tropical breakfast overlooking the turquoise lagoon. Board your scheduled return transfer to Male Airport for your onward international flight.
